About Paws of Hope

A non-profit animal charity dedicated to helping abandoned and homeless animals.

OUR STORY

Every Life Deserves Hope

Paws of Hope was created to support animals that have been abandoned, injured or neglected. Our organization works with volunteers and the local community to provide shelter, food, medical treatment and adoption opportunities.

Through our rescue and awareness programmes, we hope to encourage responsible pet ownership and create a kinder community for animals.
